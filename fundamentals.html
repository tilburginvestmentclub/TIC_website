About the Department

Understanding Companies From the Ground Up

The Fundamentals Department is TIC's core for bottom-up investment analysis. We study companies at their most granular level - from financial statements and competitive positioning to industry dynamics and macroeconomic trends.

Organized into eight specialized sector teams, each group develops deep expertise in their assigned industry. Members conduct thorough research, build detailed financial models, and present formal stock pitches to the department.

By graduation, our members have real-world experience in equity research, financial modelling, and presenting investment cases - skills directly transferable to careers in asset management, investment banking, and consulting.

Our Process

The Stock Pitch Process

Every stock pitch follows a rigorous methodology mirroring professional equity research.

01

Sector Screening

Identify compelling investment opportunities within your sector through screening and thematic analysis.

02

Deep-Dive Research

Analyse financial statements, competitive landscape, management quality, and industry dynamics.

03

Financial Modelling

Build DCF models, comparable analyses, and scenario-weighted valuations to derive a target price.

04

Pitch Presentation

Deliver a professional pitch with a clear thesis, key catalysts, risk factors, and investment recommendation.
