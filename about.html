Our Mission

Closing the gap between theory and practice

Finance courses teach students the theory. TIC teaches them how to apply it. Our members don't just read about portfolio construction — they build portfolios. They don't just study valuation methods — they pitch stocks to real investment professionals.

We believe the best way to learn investing is to do it. That means making mistakes in a safe, supportive environment, getting rigorous peer feedback, and iterating until your analysis is genuinely compelling.

Every semester, our members produce research, develop quantitative strategies, and present investment theses that would hold up in a professional setting. That's the TIC standard.

Our Story

A brief history of TIC

2019

Club Founded

TIC is founded by a group of Tilburg University finance students who wanted a more practical complement to their academic studies.

2020

Quantitative Department Launched

The quant team is formally established, introducing data-driven and algorithmic investment approaches to the club.

2021

50 Members & First Partner Network

TIC reaches 50 active members and formalizes its first cohort of industry partners, enabling company visits and guest lectures.

2023

8 Sector Teams Established

The Fundamentals Department expands to cover all eight major GICS sectors, providing full market coverage for sector analysis.

2025

80+ Members & 100+ Partners

TIC continues to grow, with a thriving alumni network, expanded partner ecosystem, and new research publications.
